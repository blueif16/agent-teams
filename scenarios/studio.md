# Northlight Studio (background for every scenario)

Harness and grader only. No agent sees this file; the simulated owner gets only its scenario's `owner` block.

Northlight is a two-person product studio. The owner takes on early-stage startup clients and runs the work
through their agent company in Agent Teams: Mira is the CEO; each client project gets a PM and at most three
experts. The company is the test company: it keeps its agents, memory and skills from one project to the next, the
way a real studio keeps its people.

How the owner works:
- Checks in a few times an hour while a project runs. Short, plain messages, often dictated.
- Decides fast. Approves a sensible plan without haggling; doesn't explain the same thing twice.
- Judges work by evidence: sources, numbers that add up, the client's own facts. Rejects claims that aren't backed.
- Changes requirements when the client does, and says so in one message.
- Uses reactions as orders: 👍 go, 🔁 redo, 📌 remember this from now on.

Clients in the scenarios (all fictional; their competitors are real, public products):
- **Tern** (`clients/tern/`): AI meeting notes for reps who meet customers in person. Scenario: market report.
- **Bookbird** (`clients/bookbird/`): online booking and reminders for small clinics. Scenario: launch kit.
