# Bookbird: product facts

Cite these as [F1]…[F10]. Use no other numbers about Bookbird.

F1. Bookbird gives each clinic an online booking page patients can use 24/7.
F2. Bookbird sends SMS and email reminders before every appointment.
F3. In a 9-clinic pilot (June to August 2026), no-shows fell from 14% to 6%.
F4. When a patient cancels, the waitlist offers the slot to the next patient automatically; in the pilot it refilled 41% of cancelled slots.
F5. Clinics can take a deposit at booking to discourage no-shows.
F6. Setup takes about 20 minutes, with a guided import of the clinic's current calendar.
F7. Bookbird syncs both ways with Google Calendar and Apple Calendar.
F8. Patients fill in intake forms on their phone before the visit.
F9. Bookbird is built for clinics with 1 to 10 practitioners: physiotherapy, dental, therapy and chiropractic.
F10. Patient data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
