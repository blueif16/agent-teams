# Bookbird (client brief)

Harness and grader only. The team gets `facts.md`, nothing else from this folder.

Bookbird is a fictional startup: online booking, reminders and a waitlist for independent clinics with 1 to 10
practitioners (physiotherapy, dental, therapy, chiropractic). It launches in two weeks and needs a landing page:
positioning, the copy, and an HTML mockup the client's developer can build from.

Buyers: clinic owners and office managers, not hospitals. Brand voice: plain and warm, short sentences, no hype.

What a good answer looks like: a teardown of at least 4 real competitor landing pages; copy in five sections whose
every claim cites a fact; a mockup that uses the copy word for word, has one primary button, and loads nothing
from the internet.
