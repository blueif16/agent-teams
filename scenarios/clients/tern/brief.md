# Tern (client brief)

Harness and grader only. The team gets `facts.md` and `packages.md`, nothing else from this folder.

Tern is a fictional startup: AI meeting notes for sales reps who meet customers face to face (field sales,
distributors, medical device reps). The rep records on their phone with the customer's consent; Tern writes the
notes and next steps into the CRM. It is not a video-call notetaker, which is where most of the market is, so the
real question for pricing is how Tern compares with the video-call tools buyers already know.

Buyers: sales managers of teams of 1 to 15 reps. Price is per user per month.

What a good answer looks like: a pricing table of at least 8 real vendors from their public pricing pages, monthly
costs for each usage profile, and a short memo that tells Tern what to charge per tier, with every price backed by
the table.
