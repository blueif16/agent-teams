# Tern: product facts

Cite these as [F1]…[F10]. Use no other numbers about Tern.

F1. Tern records in-person sales meetings on the rep's phone, with the customer's consent.
F2. Tern writes the meeting notes into Salesforce or HubSpot automatically.
F3. Notes are ready about 90 seconds after the meeting ends (pilot, 12 reps, September 2026).
F4. Tern works offline and uploads when the phone is back online.
F5. Each note lists the next steps, each with an owner and a date.
F6. Pilot reps logged 3.1× more meetings in the CRM than before Tern (12 reps, 6 weeks).
F7. Tern supports English and Spanish.
F8. Recordings are deleted after 30 days unless the team chooses to keep them.
F9. Tern is for reps who meet customers face to face: field sales, distributors and medical device reps.
F10. Tern does not join video calls; it is not a Zoom or Meet notetaker.
