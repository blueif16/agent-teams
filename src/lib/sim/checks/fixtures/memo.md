# Pricing memo: Tern tiers

## Market map

We benchmarked six plans from five vendors, normalised to what a 5-person team pays per month. The cheapest is
n8n Starter at $24 and the dearest is Lindy Business at $299.99. Most single-seat plans cost between $24 and $49.99
a month.

## Recommendation

| Tier     | Price (USD/mo) | Features                                                                 |
|:---------|---------------:|:-------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Solo     |             29 | recording; notes in 12 languages; offline mode                           |
| Team     |             79 | recording; notes in 12 languages; offline mode; shared workspace         |
| Practice |            149 | recording; Notes in 12 languages; offline mode; shared workspace; admin controls |

- Solo at $29 a month sits inside the $24–$49.99 band of single-seat plans.
- Team at $79 a month sits inside the market's p25–p75 band.
- Practice at $149 a month sits in the $99–$299.99 band, between CrewAI Basic and Lindy Business.

## Risks

| Risk                       | Mitigation                                   |
|----------------------------|----------------------------------------------|
| Annual billing hides price | Compare list prices as billed, month to month |
| Seat pricing grows fast    | Keep Team flat up to 25 people               |

Annual billing at Zapier and n8n makes their monthly price look lower than it is.
